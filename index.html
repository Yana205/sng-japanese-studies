02 · Eligibility and academic fit

Japanese entry proof and the university path

SNG says the Tokyo immigration screening criteria changed for students enrolling under Student residence status from October 2026. At application, satisfy one of these two routes. 2

Do you already hold a recognized test pass (for example JLPT N5 or higher)?

{{entryAnswer}}

A

Recognized Japanese test

What is needed

A pass certificate for an Immigration Services Agency-recognized test. The official page linked by SNG sets an A1-equivalent minimum; examples include JLPT N5 or higher and NAT-TEST level 5 or higher. 28

Meaning for an applicant without N5

A JLPT certificate is not the only accepted examination. Dates, country availability and result timing must fit the school deadline.

B

School Japanese interview

What is needed

A Japanese-language interview by the institution being applied to. The immigration page says A1-equivalent ability or higher is confirmed by test or interview. 28

Meaning for an applicant without N5

Potentially viable without a JLPT certificate, but not a waiver of Japanese ability. Ask SNG for its format, pass expectations and assessment date.

Important conflict: the April 2027 PDF checklist still asks for a 150-hour Japanese-study certificate. SNG's separate revised-rule notice says this was the old proof and now names test OR interview. Do not assume 150 hours alone is sufficient. Ask SNG to confirm whether it also wants any study-history certificate with the interview route. 12

SNG also lists a limited exception for nationals of listed countries/regions who have graduated from a foreign higher-education institution and submit graduation proof. This plan does not assume that exception applies. 2

Which SNG program fits later university or design study?

Intensive

Intended to reach advanced academic Japanese faster for university, graduate school or vocational school. SNG decides placement after a test and interview. It advises applicants not to choose Intensive above their actual ability.

13
At advanced level

Standard Advanced is for stronger general Japanese and can suit vocational-school goals. Higher Education Advanced prepares for university/graduate study, including EJU preparation or a research proposal. EJU optional classes are listed from intermediate level upward.

14
Not the same as an English-taught degree

This route is for learning Japanese, then applying separately to the next school. It is not a course in Japanese graphic-design techniques. No English test is listed in SNG's published long-term checklist, but ask SNG to confirm any applicant-specific requirement.

13
Other academic conditions

Applicants generally must have graduated from secondary education and have a financially capable sponsor. SNG says 12 years of education are normally needed for Japanese university/vocational entry; its MEXT-authorized preparatory course can address shorter national education systems. It does not automatically replace missing graduation requirements. Ask which Israeli graduation/Bagrut documents SNG accepts. 1

SNG's study charts are progression guides, not a promise of N2/N1 by a fixed date. Later university/design-school admission remains a separate language, academic and portfolio decision. 1

Study obligations and part-time work

920

class hours per year, as listed in the guide.

Class hours

Beginner through Intermediate I normally studies 13:30–17:00. Intermediate II and above normally 09:10–12:40. The student cannot choose morning or afternoon.

Part-time work

Needs separate permission, with up to 28 hours/week in ordinary term time. SNG advises avoiding work in the first 3 months.

Graduation requires completing the applied-for course, passing Beginner II or above, at least 80% total attendance, and taking the term-end graduation exam. These are SNG graduation conditions, not university acceptance conditions. 18

06 and 07 · Long-term student-visa application

Documents: the working checklist

This follows SNG's April 2027 guide, especially printed pages 8–11, and its admission page. Obtain the actual forms from SNG after screening. Original-versus-copy rules depend on nationality; the Israeli-specific list is not yet confirmed. The sponsor must be able to pay tuition and living costs, and SNG's guide does not state one universal bank-balance minimum. 15

{{docIntro}}

Issue dates

SNG's general rule is issue within the 3 months before application submission, except diplomas. The health-certificate line is inconsistent with this; use the stricter fresh-document approach until SNG clarifies.

Languages

Complete forms in Japanese or English, in capital letters except the email address and statement of purpose. Documents issued in other languages need separate Japanese translation sheets, so plan Hebrew originals with translations. SNG does not clearly specify notarization, apostille or translator certification: ask before paying for these.

Physical packet

One-sided printing/copying, no staples, no correction fluid or tape. The applicant completes forms in first person. Immigration documents are generally not returned; request return of necessary originals when registering, not after submission. Additional documents can be requested.

Sources1

Before ordering documents: ask SNG which exact Israeli school certificates/transcripts, translations, originals and sponsor records it wants. Get its written acceptance of substitutes or pending graduation results before relying on them.

Cancellation risk

The processing fee JPY 40,000 / ILS 805 is non-refundable.
